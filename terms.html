Prayer times and Qibla direction

Prayer times are calculated mathematically from your location, the date and the calculation method selected. Different authorities use different methods, and local practice, moon sighting, elevation and device settings can cause differences.

  • Times are provided as a guide. Please confirm them with your local mosque or a trusted religious authority, especially for the start of fasting and for Fajr and Maghrib.
  • The Qibla compass depends on your phone’s sensors, which can be affected by magnets, metal and electronics nearby. Calibrate the compass and verify the direction where possible.
  • The Hijri calendar is calculated and may differ by a day or two from the official start of a month declared in your country. You can adjust it in Settings.
